About me

The Iron Lady’s Path

I was born in Vilnohirsk, a small town in Ukraine’s Dnipropetrovsk region. No connections, no guarantees — just character, the gym and a huge desire to win.

My journey started with boxing and kickboxing. I won the Ukrainian junior boxing championship and became Ukrainian kickboxing champion. The striking foundation I built back then is still my biggest weapon.

In 2013 I moved to mixed martial arts and made it all the way to the UFC undefeated — 5–0. In 2015 I made my UFC debut in Kraków against Joanne Calderwood, the biggest favorite on the entire card. I broke every prediction: an armbar at 1:30 of the first round and a Performance of the Night bonus.

Over 9 years in the UFC I competed in two weight classes, beat six opponents from the world’s top league and became one of the most recognizable Ukrainian athletes in the world. For my sporting achievements and for representing Ukraine on the international stage, I have been honored with an award from the President of Ukraine.

Today I share everything I’ve learned: how professionals train, how to prepare for a fight, how to cut weight properly, and how to keep a cool head when the cage door closes.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

I’m a beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. I adapt the load to your level: with beginners we start with stance, footwork and basic strikes; with experienced athletes we work on tactics, combinations and fight preparation.

How do online sessions work?

Via video call (Zoom, Google Meet, Telegram or WhatsApp). You need a little free space and a phone or laptop on a stable surface. A bag and gloves are nice to have but not required — a lot can be done with shadowboxing and bodyweight.

What languages do you coach in?

Ukrainian, Russian and English.

Can I prepare for a real fight or competition?

Yes. That’s what the 8-week “Fight Camp” program and weight cut coaching are for. I’ll build the periodization so you peak exactly on fight day.

How do I reschedule or cancel?

Let me know at least 24 hours in advance and we’ll move your session at no cost.

How can I pay?

Any way you like: credit/debit card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, Zelle, Venmo, Cash App, Wise, Monobank or USDT crypto. You choose the method right after sending your request.

Who runs the ACL recovery sessions?

Knee rehab video sessions are run by a licensed physiotherapist, and I join at the return-to-sport stage. Your program is personalized and follows your surgeon’s recommendations.

How do I get the video workouts?

After payment I’ll send you a link with access to the videos. Access is lifetime — train any time, anywhere.
